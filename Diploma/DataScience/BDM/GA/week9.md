### Question 11 / 12

**Question:** A manufacturer of electronics has a Cost of Production of ₹15,00,000. They have opening finished goods worth ₹2,00,000 and closing finished goods worth ₹2,50,000. If the total sales revenue is ₹22,00,000, what is the Gross Profit?

**Options:**

- A. 9,50,000
- B. 6,50,000
- C. 7,00,000
- D. 7,50,000

**Selected Answer:** D. 7,50,000

**Status:** Correct (Score: 3 / 3)

**Feedback:** Gross Profit is Sales minus COGS. COGS is calculated as Cost of Production + Opening Finished Goods - Closing Finished Goods (15,00,000 + 2,00,000 - 2,50,000 = 14,50,000). Thus, Gross Profit = 22,00,000 - 14,50,000 = 7,50,000.

### Question 12 / 12

**Question:** A manufacturing unit currently buys raw materials from a wholesaler at ₹120 per unit. By switching to direct sourcing from the producer, the price drops to ₹105 per unit. If the unit requires 15,000 units of material per month, what is the annual financial gain from this cost reduction strategy?

**Options:**

- A. 18,00,000
- B. 27,00,000
- C. 2,25,000
- D. 22,50,000

**Selected Answer:** B. 27,00,000

**Status:** Correct (Score: 3 / 3)

**Feedback:** The monthly saving is (120 - 105) × 15,000 = 2,25,000. Multiplied by 12 months, the annual gain is 27,00,000.
